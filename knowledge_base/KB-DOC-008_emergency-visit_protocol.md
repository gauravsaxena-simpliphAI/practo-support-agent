# Emergency Visit and Urgent Care Protocol

- **Document ID:** KB-DOC-008
- **Topic:** emergency-visit protocol

Practo outpatient clinics operate scheduled services and do not provide emergency trauma care or intensive life support. Patients exhibiting critical warning signs—such as severe chest pain radiating to the left arm, acute stroke symptoms, severe breathing difficulty, or heavy bleeding—must immediately dial 112/108 or proceed to the nearest hospital casualty department. If a patient arrives at a clinic in unstable condition, clinic staff will provide basic first-aid stabilization and coordinate immediate ambulance transfer to a tertiary hospital.
