# Home Visit and Domiciliary Healthcare Eligibility

- **Document ID:** KB-DOC-012
- **Topic:** home-visit eligibility

Home healthcare visits by qualified general physicians and nursing personnel are reserved for geriatric patients, mobility-impaired individuals, and bedbound post-operative patients. Home visit requests must be booked at least 24 hours in advance and remain subject to physician serviceability within a 10-kilometer radius of the partner clinic hub. Emergency medical cases, complex pediatric assessments, and surgical procedures are strictly excluded from home visit services and require clinic or hospital admission.
