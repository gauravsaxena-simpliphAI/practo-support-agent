# Follow-Up Visit Discount Policy

- **Document ID:** KB-DOC-010
- **Topic:** follow-up-visit discount policy

Patients attending an in-person follow-up consultation with the same doctor within 7 calendar days of an initial completed visit receive a 50% discount on the consultation fee. Follow-up visits scheduled between day 8 and day 14 post-consultation are eligible for a 25% courtesy discount on standard clinic consultation rates. Any visit occurring after 14 days or addressing an unrelated new medical complaint is classified as a fresh primary consultation billed at standard tariff.
