# Telemedicine Consultation Eligibility

- **Document ID:** KB-DOC-007
- **Topic:** telemedicine eligibility

Telemedicine consultations are available for non-emergency follow-ups, minor acute ailments, second opinions, and routine chronic disease management across eligible specialties. Patients experiencing severe chest pain, acute respiratory distress, severe trauma, sudden neurological deficits, or high-risk pediatric conditions are strictly ineligible for teleconsultation and must visit an emergency room. All virtual visits are conducted over encrypted end-to-end video connections adhering to national Telemedicine Practice Guidelines.
