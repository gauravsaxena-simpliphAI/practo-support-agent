# Consultation Fee Structure by Specialty

- **Document ID:** KB-DOC-003
- **Topic:** consultation-fee structure by specialty

Consultation fees are tiered strictly by medical specialty and doctor clinical seniority across all partner clinics. Standard OPD consultations range from INR 400 to INR 600 for General Medicine and Pediatrics, INR 600 to INR 1,000 for Dermatology and Orthopedics, and INR 800 to INR 1,800 for Cardiology and super-specialty consultations. All published fees are all-inclusive of routine clinical examinations, vital measurements, and preliminary digital prescriptions.
