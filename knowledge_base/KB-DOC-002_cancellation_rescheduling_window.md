# Cancellation and Rescheduling Window Policy

- **Document ID:** KB-DOC-002
- **Topic:** cancellation/rescheduling window

Patients may cancel or reschedule their scheduled appointments free of penalty up to two hours prior to the booked consultation slot. Cancellations requested within less than two hours of the appointment time incur a late cancellation fee equal to 20% of the consultation charge. If an appointment is cancelled by the consulting doctor or clinic due to emergencies, patients receive an immediate 100% refund or priority rebooking at no added cost.
