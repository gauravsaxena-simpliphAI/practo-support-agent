# Prescription Refill and Renewal Policy

- **Document ID:** KB-DOC-005
- **Topic:** prescription-refill policy

Prescription refills for chronic, maintenance medications can be requested digitally through the Practo patient portal within 90 days of the last clinical visit. Controlled substances, antibiotics, narcotic analgesics, and specialized cardiac drugs cannot be refilled without an in-person or live teleconsultation review. The treating physician reviews and approves standard refill requests within 24 business hours, dispatching a digitally signed e-prescription directly to the patient's verified mobile app.
