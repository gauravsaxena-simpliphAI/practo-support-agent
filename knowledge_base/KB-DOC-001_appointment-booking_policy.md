# Appointment Booking Policy

- **Document ID:** KB-DOC-001
- **Topic:** appointment-booking policy

Patients can schedule outpatient consultations across all specialties through the Practo app, web portal, or verified clinic helplines up to 30 days in advance. Every booking requires a verified patient contact number, valid government identification, and primary reason for consultation before confirmation. A confirmed booking generates a unique appointment record ID (e.g., PRAC-1001) along with an instant SMS and email confirmation containing clinic location and doctor details.
