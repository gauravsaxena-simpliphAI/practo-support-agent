# Second Opinion Consultation Process

- **Document ID:** KB-DOC-011
- **Topic:** second-opinion process

Patients seeking an independent expert second opinion can book dedicated 30-minute review sessions with senior specialty consultants through the Practo Second Opinion Desk. Prior to the scheduled consultation, patients must securely upload prior diagnostic reports, imaging scans, and clinical case summaries for advance specialist review. The consultant provides a comprehensive written clinical evaluation within 48 hours, highlighting alternative diagnoses, updated treatment plans, and evidence-based recommendations.
