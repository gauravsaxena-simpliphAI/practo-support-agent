# Diagnostic Lab Test Turnaround Times

- **Document ID:** KB-DOC-006
- **Topic:** lab-test turnaround times

Routine diagnostic tests including complete blood counts, urine analysis, and basic metabolic panels are completed with digital reports uploaded within 12 to 24 hours of sample collection. Specialized diagnostic tests, such as histopathology, hormone panels, microbiology cultures, and advanced cardiac markers, require between 48 to 72 business hours for comprehensive pathologist review. Critical lab alerts indicating abnormal findings are flagged automatically for urgent physician review and direct telephonic patient notification.
