# Insurance Claim and Reimbursement Process

- **Document ID:** KB-DOC-004
- **Topic:** insurance-claim process

Practo partner clinics support cashless claims with major empanelled health insurance providers and Third Party Administrators (TPAs). Patients must present a valid physical insurance e-card and photo ID at the clinic billing desk at least thirty minutes prior to their consultation for eligibility pre-authorization. For non-network insurers, the clinic provides an itemized medical bill, doctor consultation summary, and digital payment receipt within 24 hours to facilitate direct patient reimbursement.
