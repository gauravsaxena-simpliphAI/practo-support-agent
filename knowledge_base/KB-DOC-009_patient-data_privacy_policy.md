# Patient Data Privacy and Confidentiality Policy

- **Document ID:** KB-DOC-009
- **Topic:** patient-data privacy policy

All electronic medical records, clinical notes, prescriptions, and communication logs are stored in encrypted databases compliant with ISO 27001, HIPAA, and Indian Digital Personal Data Protection standards. Access to patient health information is strictly role-based and granted only to the consulting doctor, assigned clinical staff, and the authenticated patient. Practo never sells, monetizes, or shares identifiable patient health information with pharmaceutical companies, advertisers, or unassociated commercial entities.
